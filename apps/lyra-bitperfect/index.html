Purpose

The recording comes first.

Music playback should not compete with the music.

Lyra BitPerfect is designed to preserve the signal, expose the real playback path and leave interpretation to the listener.

It does not attempt to improve the recording through artificial enhancement. It does not disguise processing as fidelity.

Faithfulness is the default. Choice remains yours. Just as on the finest Hi-Fi systems, Lyra BitPerfect includes a dedicated DSP Tone Control for those moments when the listener deliberately chooses a different tonal balance. It never acts automatically, never replaces the recording, and never decides on behalf of the listener.

The purpose of the instrument is not to impose a sound. It is to let the recording remain itself.

Instrument

Playback made visible.

Lyra BitPerfect treats the phone as a real audio machine, not as an empty surface for generic controls.

01

Reference playback

A playback environment designed around the original recording rather than artificial coloration.

02

DAC awareness

The active output path and connected digital audio hardware are presented as part of the instrument.

03

Signal monitoring

Visual instruments reveal level, channel relationship and playback behaviour without decorative animation.

04

Physical interaction

Controls are designed as parts of a coherent audio instrument: direct, legible and deliberate.

05

DSP Tone Control

A dedicated DSP stage recreates the classic Tone Control found on traditional Hi-Fi equipment. It offers deliberate tonal refinement whenever the listener chooses it, while reference playback always remains the application's natural starting point.
