Lyra BitPerfect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

1. DEVELOPER AND SCOPE

This Privacy Policy describes how Lyra BitPerfect (the “Application”), developed and published by Ancient BitForge, accesses and handles information on Android devices.

Lyra BitPerfect is a local high-fidelity audio player. Ancient BitForge does not operate user accounts or an application backend and does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share personal data, audio files, listening history, or user behaviour.

2. DEVICE ACCESS AND PERMISSIONS

  • Audio files and media library access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on compatible older Android versions): used only to let you select, index, display, and play audio files stored on your device. The Application does not upload, modify, or delete your audio files.
  • USB host access: used to detect and communicate with a compatible external USB digital-to-analogue converter (DAC) for supported audio playback. USB access is local to the connected device and is not used to identify or track the user.
  • Audio, foreground playback and wake-lock capabilities: used to control playback, keep active playback operating when appropriate, and manage audio output. These capabilities do not collect personal data.
  • Haptic feedback: interface controls may use Android haptic feedback. This does not collect or transmit data.

3. DATA PROCESSING AND SHARING

Audio playback, level calculations, waveform, VU meter and vectorscope processing take place locally on the device.

Lyra BitPerfect does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking cookies, behavioural profiling, or third-party data brokers. Ancient BitForge does not sell or share personal or sensitive user data.

The Application does not collect names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, IP addresses, device identifiers, payment information, or audio content for transmission to Ancient BitForge.

4. TRIAL AND LOCAL APPLICATION DATA

To manage the 21-day evaluation period, the Application stores the first-use time, most recent valid time, and local licence status on the device. Where supported, these values are protected using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences with AES-256 encryption. They are used only to calculate the trial period and limit gains from moving the system clock backwards.

The current version does not transmit trial information to Ancient BitForge and does not process payment information. If a future version introduces Google Play Billing or another service that changes data handling, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that version is distributed.

5. RETENTION AND DELETION

The Application may retain local preferences, playlists or library references, playback state, and trial information on the device for as long as the Application remains installed or its application data is retained.

Ancient BitForge does not retain this information on its servers. Users can delete locally stored application data through Android settings or by uninstalling the Application. Removing application data may reset preferences, playlists, and other local state, subject to the Application’s trial-protection mechanisms and Android backup behaviour.

6. SECURITY

Local data is handled using Android platform storage and security mechanisms. No method of storage is completely risk-free, but the Application minimises data handling by keeping its operational information on the device and avoiding transmission to an Ancient BitForge server.

7. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Lyra BitPerfect is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated when Application features, permissions, services, or legal requirements change. The revision date shown above identifies the current version.
