Lyra BitPerfect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. DEVELOPER AND SCOPE

This Privacy Policy explains how Lyra BitPerfect (the “Application”), developed and published by Ancient BitForge, accesses, processes and stores information.

Lyra BitPerfect is a high-fidelity audio player designed to process music locally on Android devices. Audio playback, music library operations and audio monitoring do not require the transmission of music files or listening history to Ancient BitForge.

The Application also uses a limited online licensing service to manage its 21-day evaluation period, verify application integrity and determine licence eligibility. This service operates through Google Firebase infrastructure.

Ancient BitForge does not operate user accounts for Lyra BitPerfect and does not use the licensing service to analyse listening habits, deliver advertising or create behavioural profiles.

2. DEVICE ACCESS AND PERMISSIONS

  • Audio files and media library access (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on compatible older Android versions): used to select, index, display and play audio files stored on the device. The Application does not upload, modify or delete users' audio files.
  • USB host access: used to detect and communicate with compatible external USB digital-to-analogue converters (DACs) for supported audio playback. USB audio operations are performed locally.
  • Audio, foreground playback and wake-lock capabilities: used to control playback, maintain active playback where appropriate and manage audio output.
  • Haptic feedback: used where supported to provide physical feedback from interface controls.
  • Device identification: the Application accesses the Android device identifier (ANDROID_ID) for licence verification and prevention of repeated trial activation. The identifier is transmitted to the licensing service, where it is transformed into a pseudonymous device key.
  • Internet connectivity: used for licence verification, application integrity checks and communication with the relevant Google services.

3. DATA PROCESSING AND SHARING

Audio playback, level calculations, waveform processing, VU meter operation and vectorscope processing take place locally on the device.

Ancient BitForge does not receive audio files, music library contents, playlists or listening history through the licensing service.

Lyra BitPerfect does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs for behavioural profiling, advertising tracking cookies or third-party data brokers. Ancient BitForge does not sell personal data.

The online licensing process involves the transmission and processing of a device identifier, integrity verification information and licence-related technical data. The purposes of this processing are to provide and maintain application access, administer the evaluation period, verify application integrity and protect the licensing service against misuse.

The licensing service uses Google Firebase, including Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore and Firebase App Check, together with Google Play Integrity. These services may process additional technical information necessary to operate and secure requests, including IP addresses, service metadata and integrity attestation information, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Information processed for licensing is not used by Ancient BitForge for advertising, analysis of music preferences or behavioural profiling.

4. TRIAL, LICENSING AND GOOGLE PLAY PURCHASES

Lyra BitPerfect offers a 21-day evaluation period. The Application uses local licence information and a server-side verification system to determine when the evaluation period begins, when it expires and whether access remains authorised.

During verification, the Application transmits an Android device identifier to the licensing service. The server applies a secret-keyed cryptographic transformation to derive a pseudonymous device key. This derived key is used to associate licence records with the device. The original Android identifier is not stored as a field in the trial records created by the reviewed licensing implementation.

The licensing service stores the date of first activation, the trial expiration date, the most recent verification date and a technical schema version. These records help maintain the original evaluation period and prevent unintended or repeated trial activation.

Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check are used to support verification of application and device integrity and protect access to the licensing service. Temporary verification challenges and request-rate controls are also used to prevent misuse.

Where supported, the Application maintains protected local licence information using Android security mechanisms. A signed offline authorisation may allow temporary operation without a new server verification while the authorisation remains valid.

Lyra BitPerfect uses Google Play Billing for paid access. Purchases and payment processing are managed through Google Play. Ancient BitForge does not receive or store users' payment card details through the Application. Google may process purchase-related information under its own applicable privacy policies.

5. RETENTION, DELETION AND DATA PROTECTION RIGHTS

The Application may retain local preferences, playlists or music library references, playback state and licence information while installed or while its application data is retained.

Users may remove locally stored application information through Android settings or by uninstalling the Application. Removing local data does not necessarily remove server-side licence records or reset the evaluation period.

Server-side trial records are maintained for licence administration, continuity of trial eligibility and prevention of repeated or unauthorised evaluation periods. They are not automatically deleted when the 21-day evaluation period expires.

Ancient BitForge will retain these records only for as long as a valid and proportionate purpose or applicable legal requirement justifies their retention, subject to periodic review and applicable data protection rights. No fixed automatic deletion period is currently configured for the trial records.

Temporary integrity challenges and request-rate records are subject to Firestore time-to-live policies. Eligible records are deleted through background processes after their configured expiration. Deletion may not occur immediately at the exact expiration time.

Where applicable, users may request access to, rectification of or erasure of personal data, restriction of processing, or object to processing. Other rights may apply depending on the circumstances and applicable law.

To make a privacy request, contact Ancient BitForge using the email address in Section 9. Please identify Lyra BitPerfect and describe your request. Ancient BitForge may ask for limited additional information where reasonably necessary to identify the relevant licence record or verify that a request concerns the appropriate device. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or identity documents unless specifically required through an appropriate procedure.

Requests will be assessed individually under applicable data protection law. Where erasure is required, the relevant information will be deleted. Where particular information must lawfully be retained, Ancient BitForge will explain the applicable grounds and any relevant limitations.

Ancient BitForge will respond without undue delay and, where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, normally within one month of receiving the request, subject to legally permitted extensions.

Individuals may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), available at https://www.aepd.es/.

6. SECURITY

Lyra BitPerfect uses Android platform security mechanisms for local information and secure communications for interactions with its licensing service.

Server-side licence identifiers are derived using a secret-keyed cryptographic function rather than storing the original Android identifier directly in the trial records.

The licensing system uses application integrity verification, access restrictions, protected server-side secrets and signed offline authorisations to reduce the risk of unauthorised access or manipulation.

Access to server-side records is managed through Google Cloud and Firebase security mechanisms. While reasonable technical measures are used to protect information, no electronic system or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Lyra BitPerfect is not directed at children. Ancient BitForge does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising, profiling or marketing purposes.

The revision date shown above identifies the current published version. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy and, where applicable, in the Google Play Data safety declaration.
