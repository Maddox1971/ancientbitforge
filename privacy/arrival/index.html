Arrival Privacy Policy

Local-first planning, notes and event resources

Effective date: 19 July 2026

1. SCOPE AND COMMITMENT

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrival accesses and handles information when you use the application. Arrival is designed as a local-first application: Ancient BitForge does not operate an Arrival account service or an Arrival content server, and does not receive the events, notes, recordings, photographs, scans or documents that you create or import into the app.

This policy applies to the Android version of Arrival distributed by Ancient BitForge through Google Play. It should be read together with any permission notice displayed by Android or by a system service when you choose to use a particular feature.

2. INFORMATION HANDLED BY ARRIVAL

Arrival can handle the following information solely to provide the functions you choose to use:

  • Planning data: event titles, dates, times and checklist elements;
  • Notes: written notes and voice recordings created inside Arrival;
  • Attachments and resources: photographs, scanned documents and files selected or created by you;
  • Preferences: the selected application language and other local preferences; and
  • Trial and licence state: the local trial and entitlement status needed to determine whether the application is available for use.

Arrival does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, postal addresses, contact lists, precise location, advertising identifiers, browsing history or authentication credentials. Ancient BitForge does not use Arrival content for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. PERMISSIONS AND USER-INITIATED ACCESS

Arrival accesses sensitive device functions only when you actively select the corresponding feature:

  • Microphone: used to record a voice note. Recording begins only after you choose the voice-note function and start recording.
  • Camera and photographs: used when you choose to capture a photograph for an event or resource. Depending on the Android version, capture may be handled by the system camera application.
  • Document scanner: used when you choose the scanner. The scanning interface is provided through Google ML Kit Document Scanner or related Google Play services.
  • Files: used through the Android system picker when you choose a document. Arrival does not scan your general file storage or import files without your selection.

The current version of Arrival does not send event notifications and does not request notification permission.

4. LOCAL STORAGE AND PROCESSING

Events, checklist elements, notes, recordings, captured images, scans, imported file copies and preferences are processed and stored locally in application-controlled storage on your device. Arrival does not automatically upload this content to Ancient BitForge.

Android, your device manufacturer, a storage provider or a device-backup service may handle information independently of Arrival when you use their services or enable device backups. Their handling is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

5. TRIAL, PURCHASES AND LICENCE VERIFICATION

Arrival provides a 21-day evaluation period. Information needed to calculate the evaluation period and remember the local entitlement state is stored on the device. This information is used only to operate the trial and determine access to the application.

If a purchase option is offered, payment processing and licence verification are provided by Google Play. Ancient BitForge does not receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. Google may provide the application with purchase and entitlement results needed to unlock or restore access. Google processes payment and account information under its own privacy policy.

6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Arrival does not include advertising SDKs or general-purpose analytics SDKs. It may interact with the following third-party or operating-system services when required by a feature:

  • Google Play: for application distribution and, if enabled, purchases and entitlement verification;
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner / Google Play services: to provide the optional scanning workflow; and
  • Android system components and compatible applications: for user-selected camera capture, file selection and opening compatible attachments.

Ancient BitForge does not control the independent data practices of Google, device manufacturers, storage providers or other applications that you choose to use.

7. DATA SHARING

Ancient BitForge does not sell, rent or broker Arrival content or personal information. Arrival does not automatically transmit your events, notes or attachments to Ancient BitForge or to advertisers.

Information may leave the device only through an action you initiate, through a system service needed to perform that action, through Google Play purchase or licence processing, or through device backup and synchronisation features that you have enabled outside Arrival.

8. RETENTION AND DELETION

Locally stored content remains on the device until you delete it using the controls available in Arrival, clear the application's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the application. When Arrival removes a locally stored attachment, it removes the copy controlled by Arrival; an original file held by another application or storage provider is not deleted.

Because Ancient BitForge does not receive or maintain an Arrival content account, it cannot remotely retrieve or delete content stored only on your device. You should keep independent backups of information that is important to you before clearing application data or uninstalling Arrival.

9. SECURITY

Arrival relies on Android application isolation and application-controlled storage to reduce unauthorised access to locally stored information. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security. Device security, screen locking, operating-system updates, backups and access granted to other applications remain under your control.

10. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Arrival is not specifically directed to children under 13, and Ancient BitForge does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an Arrival account or server. Parents and guardians should supervise access to the device and any content created locally.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

Ancient BitForge may update this policy when Arrival's functionality, permissions, third-party services or legal obligations change. The effective date shown at the beginning of the policy will be revised when a material update is published. The current version will remain available at the public policy URL stated above.
