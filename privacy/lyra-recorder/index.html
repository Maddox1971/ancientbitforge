Lyra Recorder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

1. DEVELOPER AND SCOPE

This Privacy Policy explains how Lyra Recorder (the “Application”), developed and published by Ancient BitForge, accesses, processes, stores and deletes information.

Lyra Recorder is an Android audio recording application designed for voice, field and reference recording. Microphone audio, recordings, recording markers, calibration measurements and recording settings are processed locally on the user’s device. The Application does not transmit recordings or their audio content to Ancient BitForge.

The Application also uses a limited online licensing service to manage its 21-day evaluation period, verify application integrity and determine licence eligibility. This service operates through Google Firebase infrastructure.

Ancient BitForge does not operate user accounts for Lyra Recorder and does not use the licensing service to analyse recording habits, deliver advertising or create behavioural profiles.

2. DEVICE ACCESS AND PERMISSIONS

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used to capture audio through the device microphone or a supported external audio input. Audio is processed locally for recording, signal level monitoring, waveform and spectrum visualization, gain control, high-pass filtering, limiter operation and calibration.
  • Local files and storage: Depending on the Android version, the Application uses app-specific storage, supported legacy file permissions or the Android system file picker to create, access, play and manage audio files. It does not automatically scan unrelated personal files.
  • Audio playback and monitoring: Used to reproduce recordings and, where supported, monitor the incoming audio signal. These operations are performed locally.
  • Haptic feedback: Used where supported to provide physical feedback from interface controls.
  • Device identification: The Application accesses the Android device identifier (ANDROID_ID) for licence verification and prevention of repeated trial activation. The identifier is transmitted to the licensing service, where it is transformed into a pseudonymous device key.
  • Internet connectivity: Used for licence verification, application integrity checks and communication with the relevant Google services.

Permissions are used for the functions that require them, subject to Android’s permission and device compatibility mechanisms.

3. DATA PROCESSING AND SHARING

Microphone capture, recording, signal level calculations, waveform generation, spectrum analysis, VU meter operation, gain adjustment, high-pass filtering, limiter processing, recording markers, calibration and playback take place locally on the device.

Ancient BitForge does not receive audio recordings, microphone content, recording markers, calibration measurements or locally stored audio files through the licensing service.

Lyra Recorder does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs for behavioural profiling, advertising tracking cookies or third-party data brokers. Ancient BitForge does not sell personal data.

The online licensing process involves the transmission and processing of a device identifier, integrity verification information and licence-related technical data. The purposes of this processing are to provide and maintain application access, administer the evaluation period, verify application integrity and protect the licensing service against misuse.

The licensing service uses Google Firebase, including Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore and Firebase App Check, together with Google Play Integrity. These services may process additional technical information necessary to operate and secure requests, including IP addresses, service metadata and integrity attestation information, in accordance with Google’s applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Information processed for licensing is not used by Ancient BitForge for advertising, analysis of recording habits or behavioural profiling.

When users deliberately share or open files through Android system components or third-party applications, the receiving service may process those files according to its own privacy practices.

4. TRIAL, LICENSING AND GOOGLE PLAY PURCHASES

Lyra Recorder offers a 21-day evaluation period. The Application uses local licence information and a server-side verification system to determine when the evaluation period begins, when it expires and whether access remains authorised.

During verification, the Application transmits an Android device identifier to the licensing service. The server applies a secret-keyed cryptographic transformation to derive a pseudonymous device key. This derived key is used to associate licence records with the device. The original Android identifier is not stored as a field in the trial records created by the licensing implementation.

The licensing service stores the date of first activation, the trial expiration date, the most recent verification date and a technical schema version. These records help maintain the original evaluation period and prevent unintended or repeated trial activation.

Google Play Integrity and Firebase App Check are used to support verification of application and device integrity and protect access to the licensing service. Temporary verification challenges and request-rate controls are also used to prevent misuse.

Where supported, the Application maintains protected local licence information using Android security mechanisms. A signed offline authorisation may allow temporary operation without a new server verification while the authorisation remains valid.

Clearing local application data or reinstalling the Application does not necessarily establish a new evaluation period, because the original trial registration may remain associated with the device in the licensing service.

Lyra Recorder uses Google Play Billing for paid access after the evaluation period. Access is offered through a one-time in-app purchase, without a recurring subscription.

Purchases and payment processing are managed through Google Play. Ancient BitForge does not receive or store users’ payment card details through the Application. Google may process purchase-related information under its own applicable privacy policies.

5. LOCAL RECORDINGS, MARKERS AND SETTINGS

Recordings created by Lyra Recorder are stored locally in the Application’s designated storage. They are not automatically uploaded to Ancient BitForge, Firebase or Google Cloud.

Recording markers and their associated information are stored locally with the relevant recording session where applicable.

The Application may retain local preferences such as recording format, calibration state, thresholds, recording options and interface state. These preferences are used to operate the Application.

Users may select compatible audio files through the Android system file picker for local playback or management. Lyra Recorder accesses files selected through the relevant Android mechanisms.

Deleting a recording through the Application removes the corresponding local file when Android grants the required access. Depending on storage location and Android behavior, imported or externally stored files may require separate management.

Users should maintain backups of any recordings they wish to preserve.

Local recordings, markers and settings are not used by the licensing service for device identification, advertising or behavioural profiling.

6. RETENTION, DELETION AND DATA PROTECTION RIGHTS

The Application may retain local recordings, markers, preferences, calibration settings and licence information while installed or while its application data is retained.

Users may remove locally stored application information through the Application, Android settings or by uninstalling the Application, depending on the type and storage location of the information.

Files stored outside the Application’s app-specific storage may remain after uninstallation and must be managed separately.

Removing local application data does not necessarily remove server-side licence records or reset the evaluation period.

Server-side trial records are maintained for licence administration, continuity of trial eligibility and prevention of repeated or unauthorised evaluation periods. They are not automatically deleted when the 21-day evaluation period expires.

Ancient BitForge will retain these records only for as long as a valid and proportionate purpose or applicable legal requirement justifies their retention, subject to periodic review and applicable data protection rights. No fixed automatic deletion period is currently configured for the main trial records.

Temporary integrity challenges and request-rate records are subject to Firestore time-to-live policies. Eligible records are deleted through background processes after their configured expiration. Deletion may not occur immediately at the exact expiration time.

Where applicable, users may request access to, rectification of or erasure of personal data, restriction of processing, or object to processing. Other rights may apply depending on the circumstances and applicable law.

To make a privacy request, contact Ancient BitForge using the email address in Section 10. Please identify Lyra Recorder and describe your request.

Ancient BitForge may ask for limited additional information where reasonably necessary to identify the relevant licence record or verify that a request concerns the appropriate device. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or identity documents unless specifically required through an appropriate procedure.

Requests will be assessed individually under applicable data protection law. Where erasure is required, the relevant information will be deleted. Where particular information must lawfully be retained, Ancient BitForge will explain the applicable grounds and any relevant limitations.

Ancient BitForge will respond without undue delay and, where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, normally within one month of receiving the request, subject to legally permitted extensions.

Individuals may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), available at https://www.aepd.es/.

7. SECURITY

Lyra Recorder uses Android platform security mechanisms for locally stored recordings, application information and licence data, together with secure communications for interactions with its licensing service.

Server-side licence identifiers are derived using a secret-keyed cryptographic function rather than storing the original Android identifier directly in the trial records.

The licensing system uses application integrity verification, access restrictions, protected server-side secrets and signed offline authorisations to reduce the risk of unauthorised access or manipulation.

Access to server-side records is managed through Google Cloud and Firebase security mechanisms.

Recordings and microphone audio are not transmitted to the licensing service as part of these security checks.

While reasonable technical measures are used to protect information, no electronic system or method of storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

Lyra Recorder is not directed at children. Ancient BitForge does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising, profiling or marketing purposes.

The Application does not require users to create an Ancient BitForge account or provide an email address to use the recording functions.

The technical device and licence information described in this Policy may be processed when the Application is used, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application’s features, permissions, licensing infrastructure, service providers, data practices or applicable legal requirements change.

The revision date shown above identifies the current published version. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in this Policy and, where applicable, in the Google Play Data safety declaration.
